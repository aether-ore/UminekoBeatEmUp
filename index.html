Tutorials

Mechanics Guide

Core Controls
  • WASD or Arrows move Battler around the stage.
  • J punches and K kicks. Press again during recovery to continue combos.
  • Hold J/K to charge a Stage 3 attack if you have enough Resolve.
  • L uses Special, Q holds Duo, and Punch/Kick can parry during parry windows.
Dodge / Run
  • Tap Shift to dodge with invulnerability. Hold Shift to start running.
  • Tap dodge with no direction to back-hop; tap with a direction to dodge that way.
  • Attack during a dodge hop to spend Resolve on a dodge attack that launches or ground bounces enemies.
  • Running builds dash attack timing, but getting hit while running can trip and dizzy Battler.
Advanced Movement
  • Dash attacks require timing: attack during the flash for the powered dash strike.
  • Dash Cancel lets Battler cancel an attack into a short forward burst by spending a dash gauge.
  • The dash gauge refills over time, but pauses during running, dodge invulnerability, and some stun states.
  • Goats block movement like pillars, so dodge around them instead of trying to walk through.
Combo Mechanics
  • Launches send enemies upward; ground bounces slam airborne enemies back down for extended combos.
  • Companion launches can set up Assist Combo +500 when Battler continues the juggle.
  • Technique bonuses such as Launch, Ground Bounce, and combo milestones add to the combo bank.
  • Your score is paid out when the combo ends, multiplied by defeated enemies and boosted by Perfect waves.
Score / Blessings
  • Hits and technique bonuses build your combo bank first; score is awarded when the combo ends.
  • Defeating enemies during a combo multiplies the bank, so juggling into knockouts is worth much more.
  • Perfect waves add a large bonus, then banked score fills the golden blessing meter under Score.
  • When the meter reaches the next threshold, choose Certainty, Miracles, or an item reward after the wave.
Technique Bonuses
  • Launch, Ground Bounce, Dash Cancel, Parry, and Witch Time add bonus score to the current bank.
  • 4 Hit Combo and 10 Hit Combo reward chaining hits on one enemy before they recover.
  • Assist Combo rewards Battler for continuing a juggle started by a companion.
  • Special Finish rewards defeating the final enemy or boss with Special damage.
Defensive Mechanics
  • Parry inside the correct timing window to stop attacks and gain Parry +2000.
  • Dodge through an active hitbox during invulnerability to trigger Witch Time +1000.
  • Witch Time slows enemies briefly, restores Resolve, and helps turn defense into offense.
  • Shannon can save Battler from dangerous launching attacks and grant a protective barrier.
Resolve / Specials
  • Blue Resolve Flames restore Resolve: small flames grant 10%, large flames grant 25%.
  • Resolve powers Specials and charged attacks that can destroy the witch's magical barrier.
  • Super Charge uses Resolve and dash stock to rush a target, then releases a stronger charged attack.
  • Candy Cataclysm summons konpeito for pulses, shrapnel, juggling, and a final burst.
Companions
  • Witch companions have limited wave duration. Duration appears on Plum Tea and Konpeito.
  • Human companions like Shannon and Kanon stay through boss waves and do not expire by duration.
  • Kanon follows Battler's launches and ground bounces with staged slash assists if his gauge is ready.
  • Shannon's walls and Kanon's Stage 3 can link together for longer assist chains.
Boss Basics
  • Beatrice's golden barrier blocks normal damage. Use Resolve Specials, stake parries, and key blessings to break it.
  • Stand where Beatrice's stake will land, then parry at the right time to send it back and shatter her barrier.
  • Leviathan during the tower attack can also be parried to end the pattern and break the barrier.
  • Purgatorio summons Beatrice during normal waves; parrying her attacks makes her disappear for the wave.
